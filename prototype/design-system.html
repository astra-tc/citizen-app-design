Rules

  • Phone first: one column from 360 px, touch targets at least 44 px, the app column stays 480 px wide on large screens.
  • Right to left: layout uses logical properties (inline start and end), direction icons flip, numbers stay Latin digits.
  • Focus is always visible with a 3 px focus ring; every control has a label; every image has alt text.
  • Money is always shown with its cost before it runs, and the monthly cap is visible in the wallet.
