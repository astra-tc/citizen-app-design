# Review with the Founder and three test users (A6)

Run during M2 on a phone at 360 px or wider, one session of about 20 minutes per person, the Founder watching. Open https://astra-tc.github.io/citizen-app-design/prototype/ and record what each person does and says. Findings go to docs/review-results.md with the fixes made.

## Tasks for every user
1. Start at first-run.html, name your citizen and reach the lobby (lobby.html).
2. From the lobby, open the chat (chat.html) and find what the last reply cost.
3. In services.html find a course to continue and a way to earn.
4. In wallet.html say your balance, your monthly cap and where payouts go.
5. In settings.html switch to dark and back, then switch to Arabic.
6. Repeat tasks 1 and 4 in Arabic (prototype/ar/), right to left.

## Who
### Test user 1
A new user who has never used an AI agent, English first, phone only.

### Test user 2
An Arabic first user, reading right to left, using the Arabic screens and dark mode.

### Test user 3
A user who already earns with agents, checking costs, caps and payouts.

## What we measure
Task success without help, time per task, places where the user hesitates, and anything that reads wrong in Arabic. Each finding gets a fix or a reason, then the Founder signs off.
